01 / Define the useful difference

Differentiation earns its place when it changes what buyers notice, remember, or choose—not merely when it sounds unusual inside the company.

Mark the point where the chapter distinguishes a meaningful commercial difference from novelty for its own sake.

02 / Find the memory cue

A difference has to be attached to cues people can recognize and retrieve in buying situations.

02 / The Principle

Build the difference buyers can carry into the category.

Distinctiveness becomes useful when it is concrete enough to recognize, memorable enough to retrieve, and broad enough to matter beyond a narrow audience.

One example

A project-management tool does not become distinctive by adding another abstract promise of simplicity. It becomes easier to choose when a recognizable visual system, a familiar workflow, and a clear use case consistently signal what it is for.

Deliberate practice - 15 minutes

03 / Field Notes

Four signals about making a difference legible across creative, product, and buying contexts.

Marketing Dive / 2026-11-19

Coca-Cola retreats from divisive AI holiday creative

What happened: Coca-Cola launched its 2026 holiday campaign with human-made 'Enjoy Joy' spots, moving away from the generative-AI creative used in the previous two holiday pushes after consumer backlash, while extending the campaign across TV, digital, social, experiential, packaging, and partnerships. Why it matters: The brand is protecting a familiar emotional association—holiday connection—by changing the production signal that had begun to distract from it. Distinctiveness works when the cue helps people recognize the brand rather than making the execution itself the story. Watch: Whether the return to human-made creative improves positive brand association and broad reach, rather than only pleasing the consumers who disliked the earlier AI work.

Google / 2026-11-19

Gemini turns computer use into a product behavior

What happened: Google introduced computer use in the Gemini app for Mac, allowing users to describe multi-step work across local files, desktop apps, and websites while choosing permissions, watching a live preview, and approving key actions. Why it matters: The memorable distinction is not simply that Gemini is capable; it is that the assistant can operate in a separate, observable window while the user retains control. A capability becomes easier to retrieve when it is attached to a concrete workflow and a visible safety cue. Watch: Which repeated jobs—such as form entry, dashboard-to-deck work, or batch listings—become habitual enough to make 'computer use' a recognizable reason to choose Gemini.

Walmart / 2026-11-19

Walmart makes commerce capability part of its distinction

What happened: Walmart reported 23% growth in U.S. e-commerce, nearly 50% growth in marketplace sales, 36% growth in Walmart Connect advertising excluding VIZIO and Vibe, and a 129% year-over-year increase in U.S. Sparky usage. Why it matters: The figures show a retailer making its difference legible through a connected system of value, convenience, fulfillment, advertising, marketplace supply, and agentic shopping—not through a single abstract promise. The cue is stronger because it appears across several buying moments. Watch: Whether faster delivery and agentic discovery expand penetration among ordinary category buyers, or mainly deepen usage among Walmart's existing digital customers.

The Associated Press / 2026-11-19

Slower retail growth raises the value of a clear reason to choose

What happened: AP reported that Walmart raised its outlook for the year after a solid quarter, with e-commerce helping, even as U.S. store comparable sales declined and broader October retail growth slowed from the prior month while shoppers faced high prices. Why it matters: When budgets tighten, brands cannot assume that attention or preference will carry them through comparison. A distinct cue has to connect to a practical reason to buy—value, convenience, speed, or confidence—at the moment the buyer is deciding. Watch: Whether holiday winners make that reason visible early enough for shoppers to remember it across channels and late-season price pressure.

05 / The Practice

Make the distinction memorable enough to travel.

The aim is not to be different in a vacuum. It is to give the market a clear association that can survive clutter, comparison, and the ordinary speed of buying.
