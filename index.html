02 / The Principle

Measure the memory that makes a later choice possible.

Advertising earns its place when it builds a recognizable, retrievable association that can enter the buyer's mind before the next purchase—not only when it captures an immediate click or sale.

One example

A household brand may not convert every viewer today, but a consistent distinctive cue can make it easier to recall when the buyer later encounters the need, shelf, search result, or recommendation that opens the category.

Deliberate practice - 15 minutes

Pick one advertisement and write the category entry point it should own, the memory cue that links it to the brand, and the leading indicator you would monitor before sales appear.

Disney is turning holiday reach into a connected path to action

What happened: Disney's holiday advertising bookings across streaming and linear are pacing 20% ahead of the same point last year. Its StorySync approach joins sponsorships, in-show integrations, custom content, social and influencer work, commerce, experiential activity, and halo enhancements, with new shoppable activations being added. Why it matters: The campaign is designed to leave more than an impression: a shared cultural moment, a repeated brand cue, and a route to transaction reinforce one another across contexts. The useful memory may be built by the sequence, not by any single ad unit. Watch: Whether the added commerce layer preserves the emotional and cultural association that earns attention, or makes the experience feel like a series of disconnected conversion prompts.
