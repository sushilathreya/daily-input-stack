02 / The Principle

Own the decision the service simplifies.

A service becomes easier to choose when it is attached to one recognizable situation and one useful comparison. The position should reduce interpretive work before the feature list begins.

One example

A bookkeeping service should not lead with every task it can perform. It might own the position of the weekly cash view that lets a small-business owner decide what to do next, then use its features as proof of that promise.

03 / Field Notes

Four current signals about making a service easier to find, compare, and trust.

OpenAI / 2026-11-24

OpenAI makes a concrete biological job the frame for model capability

What happened: OpenAI reported that its GPT-6 models designed protein structures and sequences through reasoning and code, with lab tests finding binding evidence across five targets, while specialized models remained part of some workflow steps. Why it matters: The capability becomes easier to understand when attached to a specific design job and a visible test. The concrete frame carries more decision value than a general claim of intelligence, while the stated limitation keeps the position credible. Watch: Watch whether later work turns the demonstrated component capability into a reliable end-to-end biology workflow, and whether users remember the model for a job rather than for a benchmark adjective.

Marketing Dive / 2026-11-25

Holiday brands are using handmade craft to make humanity legible

What happened: Marketing Dive reported that Coca-Cola, Walmart, and Levi's are leaning into handmade craft, creators, and real people as consumers grow weary of AI-generated advertising. Why it matters: The contrast is doing positioning work: the brands are making human connection and visible effort the comparison that separates their holiday messages from polished sameness. The medium becomes evidence for the association. Watch: Watch whether craft produces a distinctive brand memory or becomes another repeatable aesthetic, and whether the human signal connects to a specific customer situation rather than serving as decoration.

Retail Dive / 2026-11-24

Retailers are treating operational resilience as part of the offer

What happened: Retail Dive reported that retailers were entering Black Friday after widespread outages across sectors, prompting caution around major system changes, promotions, stock availability, and data safety. Why it matters: During peak demand, reliability becomes a choice criterion rather than invisible infrastructure. A retailer can be remembered not just for the deal, but for making the high-stakes shopping moment feel dependable. Watch: Watch which retailers turn resilience into a visible customer promise through stock accuracy, checkout continuity, and clear recovery paths when systems fail.

TechCrunch / 2026-11-24

Walmart lets an outside AI agent carry the shopping comparison

What happened: TechCrunch reported that Walmart partnered with Meta so shoppers can use the Muse AI agent to find and purchase Black Friday products, with price comparison and user controls over shopping history and spending. Why it matters: The retailer is making convenience and comparison available through an intermediary instead of requiring shoppers to begin inside Walmart's own interface. The position now has to survive when another agent explains the choice. Watch: Watch whether Walmart's product facts, price confidence, and privacy controls are strong enough for an external agent to recommend it, and whether the convenience changes who owns the customer relationship.

05 / The Practice

Make the choice legible before making the claim larger.

A clear position gives the buyer a useful shortcut: this is the service for that situation, compared in that way, for that reason.
