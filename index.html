01 / Locate the pressure

A crisis gathers the forces that have been building and makes them impossible to ignore.

Name the desire, opposition, and cost that narrow the protagonist's room to act.

02 / Separate obstacle from choice

A difficult event is not yet a crisis. The crisis arrives when the protagonist must choose among consequential paths.

Mark what each option protects, sacrifices, or makes newly possible.

03 / Read character through action

The pressured choice reveals priorities more clearly than explanation or self-description.

Notice which value the action makes visible and what consequence the protagonist accepts.

04 / Map one live decision

Choose one story, pitch, or customer decision. Write the pressure, available options, decisive choice, and resulting change in four lines.

Strengthen the consequence until the choice teaches the audience something that an easy resolution could not reveal.

02 / The Principle

Use pressure to make values visible.

A crisis concentrates desire and opposition until the protagonist must choose. The choice matters because its cost turns an abstract value into visible action.

One example

A product story becomes more persuasive when the customer must choose between preserving a familiar workflow and accepting a new constraint that unlocks a more important outcome.

Deliberate practice - 15 minutes

Choose one story, offer, or product decision. Write the desire, the opposing pressure, the two or three costly options, the choice, and the consequence. Remove any option that carries no real tradeoff.

A product fragment on turning capability into behavior

This fragment is useful because it names the behavioral question behind a new capability. The important test is not whether the feature is impressive, but whether it changes a repeated workflow enough for the user to adopt it.

It would be quite the irony if AI makes most people dumber and unable to think by themselves, the same way social media made most people lonelier, and the same way a safer and better society made most people physically and mentally weaker.

05 / The Practice

Make the choice carry the meaning.

Do not add pressure for its own sake. Build the decision until the action reveals what matters, changes the situation, and leaves a consequence the audience can feel.
