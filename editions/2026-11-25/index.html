Apprenticeship Note

02 / The Principle

Position the service around the decision it clarifies.

A service earns a useful position when it helps a buyer understand one important choice faster than the alternatives do. The position should organize the comparison before the feature list begins.

One example

A bookkeeping service can stop presenting itself as a bundle of reports and instead own the position of the weekly cash-clarity service for owners who need to know what they can safely commit to.

Deliberate practice - 15 minutes

Choose one service. Write the customer decision it should make easier, the alternative currently shaping the comparison, the one association you want the buyer to remember, and the proof that supports it. Remove claims that do not strengthen that position.

03 / Field Notes

What the field is teaching now.

Campaign Asia / 2026-11-24

Heritage alone is no longer closing the sale

What happened: Campaign Asia's Nov. 24 top story argues that global brands are testing the limits of heritage: Nike has dropped out of the S&P 100, Gucci is in its third year of falling sales, and Puma has posted a loss. Brand strategists are examining what Starbucks and Adidas are doing differently. Why it matters: The useful position is not 'we use AI' but the particular shopping decision AI helps with, while the brand earns memorability through a credible human distinction. Utility and association are doing different jobs. Watch: Watch whether heritage brands can attach their history to a current customer choice, rather than treating recognition itself as a sufficient reason to buy.

Retail Dive / 2026-11-23

Black Friday is becoming a season-long comparison problem

What happened: Retail Dive reports that 182.7 million consumers are expected to shop over Thanksgiving weekend, down more than 2% year over year, while 60% had already begun holiday shopping by early November. The report also says 59% plan to use AI for shopping tasks such as recommendations, gift ideas, budgeting, and comparison shopping. Why it matters: When offers arrive early and shoppers compare across retailers with software, a retailer needs a sharper reason to be chosen than simply having a sale. The service position shifts toward the decision it makes easier: value, convenience, confidence, or experience. Watch: Watch which retailers can turn an extended promotion calendar into a simple, memorable shopping promise instead of making customers perform the comparison themselves.

TechCrunch / 2026-11-19

Gemini is being positioned as an agent that completes desktop work

What happened: TechCrunch reports that Gemini's Mac app can navigate native apps and websites to complete multistep tasks, with a live preview and user controls to pause, stop, or approve actions. Google frames the feature around tedious work such as filling forms and organizing files. Why it matters: The product is being positioned around moments of reduced effort rather than around model capability. That makes the comparison legible: the phone that completes a small task versus the phone that merely exposes another feature list. Watch: Watch whether users remember 'computer use' as a category or simply as a reliable answer to a repeated desktop situation.

TechCrunch / 2026-11-24

AI products are racing to own a concrete job

What happened: TechCrunch's November coverage shows AI products clustering around specific jobs rather than one undifferentiated assistant pitch, including agents for coding changes, commerce, desktop work, and market predictions. Why it matters: The strategic move is a position before it is a feature: own a situation with a full workflow, then let the capabilities support the association. A narrow category can make a new service easier to compare and remember. Watch: Watch whether specialized assistants win through better completion of one journey or get pulled back into the generic-assistant comparison as their feature set expands.

05 / The Practice

Make the comparison do the selling.

Do not describe every capability equally. Give the service one clear situation, one useful comparison, and one defensible association that makes the next choice easier.
