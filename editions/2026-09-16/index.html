You cannot get there from here.

Apprenticeship Note

Strategy begins when you admit that the desired position may be unreachable from the present one. The honest question is not how to shout harder, but which move changes the route.

Chapter Reader

Read this chapter for constraint, not inspiration. Mark the moments where Ries and Trout separate effort from leverage, where a leader's position changes the route available to a challenger, and where the examples show that a new claim cannot simply leap over the category's existing mental structure. Then write down the position you want, the position you currently occupy, and the move that could connect them honestly.

01 / The desired destination does not erase the starting point

Ries and Trout begin with an ordinary impossibility: a destination can be close in abstract distance yet unreachable by the route available from where you are. In markets, ambition is often mistaken for a route.

The useful discipline is to name the present position before designing the message. A brand cannot persuade its way out of facts that the buyer already uses to classify it.

02 / More force cannot solve a structural problem

The chapter is severe about the limits of the can-do instinct. More advertising, more features, and more determined language do not automatically create the credibility or memory structure required for a different position.

This is a distinction between execution and strategy. Execution amplifies a route; it does not manufacture one when the route itself is blocked.

03 / Leaders change the geometry of competition

A dominant brand does not merely own more share. It occupies a reference point that changes how every challenger is interpreted. The challenger therefore inherits a problem of comparison before it gets a chance to explain itself.

The question becomes what the leader cannot credibly say, serve, or become. A route opens when the challenger chooses a position that is meaningful to the buyer and structurally distinct from the leader.

04 / The alternative is a credible route, not a louder claim

Ries and Trout's answer is not surrender. It is to change the frame: find the contrast, category, or point of entry that makes the offer legible from the prospect's existing mental map.

A repositioning move works when it alters what the buyer compares, not merely when it adds another benefit to the same crowded comparison.

05 / Strategy is the choice of what not to attempt

The chapter turns positioning into a test of restraint. If the desired position requires the market to forget too much, believe too large a leap, or ignore an entrenched reference point, the strategy is asking language to do the work of reality.

Stop with a route-to-position map: current place, desired place, blocking fact, credible contrast, and the first proof that would let a buyer take one step in that direction.

02 / The Principle

When the route is blocked, change the route before increasing the force.

A positioning problem is often misdiagnosed as a communications problem. First identify the present mental position and the comparison that controls it. Then choose a credible contrast that changes the buyer's route to understanding.

Example

A small accounting product cannot credibly present itself as the most complete platform against an entrenched category leader. It may instead own the simpler route for independent consultants who want their books closed without learning enterprise software.

Deliberate practice - 15 minutes

Write five lines: current position, desired position, blocking fact, credible contrast, and the proof a buyer would need before moving one step. Remove every claim that depends on the buyer forgetting the current category leader.

03 / Field Notes

What the field is teaching now.

OpenAI / 2026-09-10

Financial AI is being sold through evidence and provenance, not just model intelligence

What happened: OpenAI introduced ChatGPT for Financial Services with built-in data from Daloopa, PitchBook, and LSEG News, plus granular citations intended to let bankers trace figures and claims back to their sources. Why it matters: In a high-stakes workflow, trust is part of the product design. The differentiator is not only what the model can answer, but whether the surrounding system makes its evidence inspectable enough for a professional to act. Watch: Whether source-level citations become a buying requirement across regulated enterprise AI, and how often users actually inspect them before approving consequential work.

Google / 2026-09-10

Gemini is entering the desktop through a shortcut and a familiar task promise

What happened: Google launched a Gemini app for Windows that opens with Alt + Space and can draft documents, manage tasks, create images, and pull information from Google apps without leaving the desktop. Why it matters: The adoption story is a reduction in switching cost. Rather than asking users to learn a new destination, Google places the assistant beside an existing workflow and lets repeated convenience prove the value claim. Watch: Whether the shortcut becomes a durable habit or remains a novelty once users encounter permission, privacy, and context limits across their desktop work.

Marketing Dive / 2026-09-11

Cadbury turns generosity into a physical object, social series, and participation mechanic

What happened: Cadbury launched its largest U.S. campaign through a Luar and Nylon fashion-week collaboration, including a crystal-studded clutch designed to carry chocolate for sharing, a hero spot, an episodic social comedy series, and a nomination sweepstakes. Why it matters: The value claim gains force because the idea appears in multiple behaviors: the object makes sharing tangible, the story dramatizes it, and the sweepstakes asks people to name someone generous. The campaign gives the brand promise several ways to be enacted. Watch: Whether the unusual object and episodic format create durable association beyond the event, especially when the clutch itself is not sold and only five were made.

TechCrunch / 2026-09-11

LOOQ is trying to make out-of-home attention measurable like a digital click

What happened: TechCrunch reported that LOOQ, a Startup Battlefield regional finalist, is building camera-based hardware and an attention API to measure who actually sees billboards and other out-of-home advertising. Why it matters: The product attacks a credibility gap in physical advertising: the claim is not that a placement exists, but that someone paid attention to it. Measurement becomes the costly proof that can change how the medium is bought and evaluated. Watch: Whether advertisers trust attention estimates enough to shift budgets, and whether the metric clarifies real exposure or simply creates a new proxy that needs its own validation.

Ram Ahluwalia CFA, Lumida / following / 2026-09-16

A product fragment on turning capability into behavior

This fragment is useful because it names the behavioral question behind a new capability. The important test is not whether the feature is impressive, but whether it changes a repeated workflow enough for the user to adopt it.

Thought Experiment: What if AGI Becomes the Ultimate Midtwit? It’s fun to imagine AGI scenarios: from super abundance to dystopian. But… what if AI gets progressively smarter with more data and compute… …but never achieves Einstein / Newton level insight? In other words, AGI becomes the ultimate midtwit. The midtwit to end all midtwits. In that world, we throw more and more compute and we get better and better execution, analysis, automation, task delegation — but no Steve Jobs, Elon Musk, or Travis like creative potential. No AI designed fusion reactors, no AI theory of quantum gravity, no AI decoding of the Sumerian tablets. (No AI religions either.) Instead, we get a world of steady incrementalism, and productivity growth. Intelligence becomes a utility on tap. In that world, AI does what you want when you want. The AI gives you the conventional wisdom every time. Perfectly. MidTwit AGI never achieves dramatic breakthrus, except by brute force, but is exceptional at engineering and technique. Humans are looking at abundance and apocalyptic scenarios and under-exploring MidTwit AGI outcome. Wouldn’t that be an ironic and funny conclusion?
