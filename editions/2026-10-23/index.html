Apprenticeship Note

A disruptive opportunity can be real and still lose inside the organisation built to serve the current business. Give the new market a structure whose economics and values let it be judged fairly.

01 / The core business has a rational veto

The established business is not failing to understand the disruptive opportunity by accident. Its customers, margins, processes, and resource-allocation rules make the opportunity look unattractive.

Study the difference between an individual refusing to see a threat and a system whose definition of a good opportunity excludes the threat.

02 / Give the new market its own logic

A disruptive initiative needs a structure that can value small markets, simpler products, uncertain demand, and different margins without being measured as a disappointing version of the core business.

Notice what must be separated so the new team can learn, and what must remain shared so it can still use the parent company's resources intelligently.

03 / Design the decision process before the crisis

The organisation should decide in advance how a disruptive opportunity will be funded, staffed, measured, and protected from premature comparison with the incumbent business.

Choose one live opportunity and write the metric, owner, budget, and learning horizon that would let it be judged on the basis of the market it is trying to create.

02 / The Principle

Let the opportunity be judged by the market it is creating.

A new-market idea often looks irrational when measured by the core business's customers, margins, and processes. Give it a structure with the resources and values needed to discover whether its different tradeoff can become valuable.

One example

A mature analytics company creates a small independent team for a low-cost, self-serve product. The team shares core data infrastructure but owns its roadmap, pricing tests, and success measure: repeat use among new customers rather than immediate enterprise revenue.

Deliberate practice - 15 minutes

Choose one disruptive opportunity your organisation is considering. Write the core metric that would reject it, the customer it is meant to serve, the independent structure it needs, the shared resource it should borrow, and the first evidence that would justify another experiment.

03 / Field Notes

Four current signals on how new markets are being made legible through separate products, channels, and decision systems.

OpenAI / 2026-10-21

ChatGPT Ads expands discovery into 26 more markets

What happened: OpenAI said ChatGPT Ads is expanding to 26 additional countries, bringing availability to 89 countries, after more than 100,000 advertisers used the platform. Ads appear on Free and Go plans while paid plans remain ad-free. Why it matters: The move separates a new commercial system from the subscription core while using the same conversational surface. It is a live example of designing a distinct value proposition—discovery during consideration—rather than forcing advertising to imitate a conventional page or feed. Watch: Watch whether clear separation from answers and privacy boundaries make the new market trustworthy enough to grow without weakening the product's core decision utility.

OpenAI brings frontier models into Google Cloud's existing buying system

What happened: OpenAI and Google Cloud announced that OpenAI models and Codex agentic workflows will come to Google Cloud's Model Garden, with access controls, data governance, and purchase through Google Cloud Marketplace planned for general availability in the coming weeks. Why it matters: Adoption is being routed through the environment enterprises already trust and budget for. Watch: Watch whether model choice inside an established cloud workflow produces durable usage, or whether governance and procurement friction still keep experimentation separate from production.

Google / 2026-10-20

Google gives small businesses an AI hub built around their existing tools

What happened: Google announced a Gemini business landing page, brand-style controls, financial insight tools, templates, and connections to Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Workspace, Shopify, Canva, Dropbox, and other apps for small business owners. Why it matters: This is a product strategy signal: the new offer is made legible as a dedicated operating place for the small-business job, not as a general-purpose model asking owners to invent a workflow. Its value is easier to test when the tools, context, and audience are grouped around one market. Watch: Watch whether the hub creates repeated business routines and measurable customer outcomes, rather than becoming another layer that owners must learn before they can get work done.

OpenAI / 2026-10-20

OpenAI positions its life-sciences acquisition as a separate operating capability

What happened: OpenAI announced the acquisition of Orchestra Bio, describing a combination of AI-powered operations and life-sciences R&D workflows to help biopharma teams accelerate drug development and advance new treatments. Why it matters: Entering a specialized value network requires more than pointing the general product at a new customer. A dedicated capability can learn the domain's evidence, processes, and buying logic while still drawing on the parent's platform and research resources. Watch: Watch whether the new unit retains enough domain-specific autonomy to learn what biopharma customers value before being measured by the economics of the general AI business.

05 / The Practice

Build the conditions in which the new market can become legible.

When the core business cannot value an opportunity, change the organisation and the measure before concluding that the opportunity is weak.
